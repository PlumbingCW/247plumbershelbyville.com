Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call Shelbyville 24/7 Plumbing and how we use it. Our service runs by phone, so there is little to collect. It applies in 2026 and may be updated.

Talk to a plumber

Calls and call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you call, we may learn your phone number, your name if you give it, your address and what is wrong with your plumbing. We use that to send a plumber, to quote a price, and to improve how calls are handled.

Information you give us

We only know what you tell us on the phone or in an email you send to contact@247plumbershelbyville.com. This website has no accounts, no contact fields and no chat, so it does not ask you to type personal details.

How information is used

  • To dispatch a licensed plumber to your address.
  • To agree a price with you before work starts.
  • To follow up on a job you asked us to do.
  • To train the people who answer calls.

Phone and web services

Calls may pass through phone and call-routing services that connect you to dispatch. Your browser may also share ordinary technical details, such as device type, with the site's hosting service when a page loads.

Questions about your information

If you want to ask what we hold about you or have a question about this policy, email contact@247plumbershelbyville.com and include the phone number you called from. Please do not email emergencies, since the phone line is the way to reach a plumber.
