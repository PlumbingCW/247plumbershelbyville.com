How to find the right plumbing service

Start with the symptom. Water in the wrong place points to leak detection or burst pipe repair. A tub that gurgles or a floor drain that bubbles points to drain cleaning or sewer line cleaning, and a cold shower points to water heater repair.

Planned work sits further down the list. Water heater installation, repiping and basement waterproofing are jobs you schedule, while a failed tank or a flooded pit is a same-day call. Businesses can go straight to commercial plumbing.

Shelbyville's older housing stock, a 30 inch frost line and two rivers that meet in town shape most of these jobs. Whatever you pick, the price is agreed before work starts. Prices depend on the pipe, the access and the parts, so each page lists its own cost factors.

Plumbing work is held to the Indiana Plumbing Code, which the City of Shelbyville lists as the 2012 edition. Any digging starts with a call to Indiana 811 at least two business days ahead.
