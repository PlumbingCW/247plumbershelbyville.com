What to have ready when you call

  • The street address and town, plus the nearest cross street if you can.
  • What you see, hear or smell: running water, gurgling drains, a sewage smell, a pilot light that is out.
  • Whether you can reach the main water valve and whether water is still flowing.
  • Whether the home is on a public sewer or a septic system, if you know.
  • The age and type of the water heater, if the call is about hot water.

While you wait

Close the main water valve if water is running, and shut off power to a leaking water heater. Move rugs and electronics out of the wet area. If you smell gas, do not touch switches, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility from outside.
